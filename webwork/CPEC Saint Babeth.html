About Us

CPEC Saint Babeth is a private school located in Kigali, Rwanda. We offer Ordinary Level education (S1, S2, S3) and technical training.

Our technical programs include Software Development and Multimedia at levels L3, L4, and L5.

  • Software Development (L3, L4, L5)
  • Multimedia Studies (L3, L4, L5)
